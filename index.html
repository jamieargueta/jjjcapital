Construction Loans

For many homebuilders, obtaining a construction loan from a traditional bank can be a tedious, frustrating, and time-consuming process — often not worth the effort. JJJ Capital fills this gap by providing a more efficient and flexible private lending alternative.

01

Loan Evaluation Process

When a builder approaches JJJ Capital for a construction loan, we conduct a thorough review of both the builder and the proposed project. This includes the builder's experience and track record, details of the subject property and surrounding neighborhood, home size and construction plans, construction budget and draw schedule, projected home value upon completion, and desired loan term (typically 12–18 months).

02

Investor Notification

If, after review, JJJ Capital determines that the project is a suitable lending opportunity, it will notify its investor pool via email of the upcoming private lending opportunity. The complete investment package will be available for investors to review on this website.

03

Oversight & Inspections

For every loan, JJJ Capital participates with a minimum 10% investment of the total loan amount. JJJ Capital also oversees the loan throughout its duration, which includes conducting regular on-site inspections during construction and verifying progress before each builder-requested draw to ensure work aligns with the construction schedule.

04

Investor Participation & Funding

When an investor chooses to participate, the investor sends funds directly to the title company. This ensures that each investor is named on both the Deed of Trust and the Promissory Note for their respective share of the investment, with JJJ Capital named as the ownership of interest for the remaining undistributed loan amount. If new investors participate in subsequent draws, they will be assigned a proportional share of the Deed of Trust and Promissory Note to reflect their contribution.

05

Interest Payments & Loan Repayment

On the monthly anniversary of the initial loan funding (first draw), the builder makes interest payments to the account servicing company. This servicing company collects payments from the builder and distributes each investor's share of the interest accordingly. Investors continue to receive monthly interest payments until the loan is fully repaid — either through the sale of the completed home or a refinance with another lender. When the loan is paid off in full, the account servicing company disburses the investor's principal and any final interest owed directly to the investor.
